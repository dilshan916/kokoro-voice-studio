Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kokoro Voice Studio?

Kokoro Voice Studio is a state-of-the-art online AI text-to-speech workstation powered by the lightweight Kokoro-82M neural model. It generates natural, human-like speech across 60 voices in English, Japanese, French, Korean, Mandarin, Spanish, Hindi, Italian, and Portuguese.

Is Kokoro Voice Studio free to use?

Yes! Kokoro Voice Studio includes a generous free monthly quota of 30,000 characters with complete access to all 60 voices, audio mastering presets, and SRT subtitle export. Users can also upgrade to Unlimited Pro with VIP codes.

Can I use generated speech for YouTube, TikTok, and Podcasts?

Yes, all audio generated by Kokoro Voice Studio can be exported as studio-quality 24kHz MP3 or WAV files, along with synchronized .SRT subtitle files ready to import directly into CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve.
